# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to Learners. This glossary covers the language used for buying courses and getting money back.

## Language

### People and buying

**User**:
An account that can sign in. It has one role: Learner, Instructor or Admin.
_Avoid_: Account, member

**Learner**:
A User who buys and takes courses.
_Avoid_: Student, customer

**Purchase**:
One Learner buying one course, at the amount they paid on that day.
_Avoid_: Order, transaction

**Enrollment**:
A Learner's access to a course. A Purchase or a claimed team seat creates one.
_Avoid_: Purchase (they are not the same thing), subscription

### Refunds

**Refund**:
A Learner's request to get back the money paid for one Purchase. It is Requested, then either Approved or Declined. Only an Approved Refund sends money back.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, cancel my purchase, reversal, chargeback, refund request (as a separate concept)

**Requested**:
The status of a Refund the Learner has raised that nobody has approved or declined yet. Learners see it as "Refund requested".
_Avoid_: Pending, open

**Approved**:
The status of a Refund that has been accepted. The money goes back to the Learner.

**Declined**:
The status of a Refund that has been turned down. No money goes back.

**Refund Amount**:
The amount the Learner paid for the Purchase, whatever the course's price is today.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, list price

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a Purchase, including the whole of the fourteenth day. A Refund can only be requested inside it.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
Whether a Learner may request a Refund for a Purchase. It requires all of these:
- the Refund Window is still open
- the Purchase has never had a Refund, whatever its status
- the Learner has completed less than half of the course's lessons
- the Learner has watched less than two hours of it
- the Purchase is not a team seat someone has already claimed

**Chargeback**:
A dispute the Learner raises with their bank or card issuer to force money back. It happens outside Cadence and is never the same thing as a Refund.
_Avoid_: Using this word for a Refund
